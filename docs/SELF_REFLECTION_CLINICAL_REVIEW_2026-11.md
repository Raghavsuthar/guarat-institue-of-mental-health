# Self-Reflection Tools: Clinical Review and Release Gate

**Review date:** 4 November 2026  
**Status:** Engineering safety corrections in progress; not a substitute for clinical, licensing, or translation approval.

## Classification shown in the application

### Recognized screening instruments
PHQ-9, GAD-7, ASRS-v1.1 Part A, DAST-10, and SCOFF are identified as recognized instruments. Their current app adaptations still require a question-by-question source comparison and licensing/permission review before the site claims faithful reproduction or validated local-language versions. A positive screen is not a diagnosis.

### Adapted educational reflections
- Mood pattern reflection: nine adapted yes/no questions; not the MDQ. Do not apply MDQ scoring or call it a bipolar screen.
- Alcohol use reflection: adapted questions; not AUDIT-C. Its current shared response anchors do not reproduce AUDIT-C's item-specific options or scoring. Do not show a clinical cut-off or risk category.
- PTSD symptom reflection: five adapted questions; not PC-PTSD-5. Do not apply a validated screening threshold.
- Postpartum mood reflection: five adapted questions; not EPDS. Do not apply EPDS scoring or label it postpartum depression screening.

These tools remain available for organizing experiences and discussion only. No numeric score or severity category is shown.

### Pending review
Sleep difficulty reflection: seven items are currently shown with generic anchors. This is not an accurate reproduction of the seven-item ISI response structure. The ISI should not be scored or represented as the ISI until exact item-specific anchors and reuse permission have been confirmed. It is currently an unscored educational reflection.

## Corrections in this engineering pass
- PHQ-9 item 5 wording corrected to appetite loss or overeating; severity labels split at 15–19 and 20–27.
- GAD-7 item 4 narrowed to difficulty relaxing to avoid overlap with item 5 restlessness.
- DAST-10 item 3 remains reverse-scored (Yes = 0, No = 1); score bands are separated into 0, 1–2, 3–5, 6–8, and 9–10.
- Adapted and pending-review tools do not display a clinical score or clinical interpretation.
- Self-harm endorsements in PHQ-9 item 9 and postpartum reflection item 5 surface immediate support before the user completes the questionnaire. If there is imminent danger, users are directed to 112 / nearest emergency department; Tele-MANAS 14416 is also shown.

## Required before public claims or release sign-off
1. Compare every recognized instrument item, response option, recall period, scoring rule, and interpretation against the authoritative source version.
2. Confirm permissions/licensing for public reproduction, including translations and digital use.
3. Obtain fluent Gujarati and Hindi clinical-language review; current localization completeness does not establish semantic equivalence or psychometric validation.
4. Have a qualified clinician review crisis wording, escalation paths, postpartum safety language, and referral guidance.
5. Do not claim psychometric validation for any translation without a proper validation study.

## Known limits
This pass does not independently validate any instrument, authorize copyrighted reproduction, or certify translations. The app must remain educational and non-diagnostic.
